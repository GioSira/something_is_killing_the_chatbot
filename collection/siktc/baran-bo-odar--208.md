# Baran bo Odar

**Baran bo Odar** is a German co-showrunner, executive producer, and writer on the Netflix original supernatural drama, *Something Is Killing the Children*.

## Biography

Odar and his wife, Jantje Friese, are best known for creating the hit Netflix science fiction series *Dark*, which ran for three seasons before coming to a pre-planned end. In the years that followed, the duo announced their next big production at Netflix, *1899*, a multilingual mystery thriller that was also planned to run for three seasons but was canceled prematurely by the streaming service.

After renewing their first-look overall deal with Netflix, Odar and Friese were tapped to develop a series adaptation of the *Something Is Killing the Children* comic series.
