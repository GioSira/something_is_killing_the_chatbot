 Ben is a motel owner in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin .

 History 
 Ben was a motel owner in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the 2020 Archer's Peak incident . Erica Slaughter rented a room from him, but after she angrily brushed off a phone call, and he began to hear weird noises from her room, he called the police to take her away. He met with Sheriff Cavanaugh , who later escorted Erica out. 

 Physical appearance 
 Ben is an elder Caucasian man with gray hair.

 Personality 
 Ben is very gruff and direct. He discerned that Erica was trouble and called the police on her. He is known to be a smoker.
