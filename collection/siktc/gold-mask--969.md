# Gold Mask

| “ | If the central tenet of the Order of St. George is that one most become a monster to hunt monsters, it followed that only one who has killed dragons could take the title of Dragon. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Slaughter |  |

**The Dragons** of the Order of St. George, also known as **Gold Masks,** are the leaders of each House of The Order of St. George. Wearing a metallic golden mask, shaped like animalistic fangs, the Dragons are appointed by Council and serve The Dragon King as they control their houses and herald communication between others.

## Description

Gold Masks are the leaders of each House. They are given great respect, and final say in every major decision of the House. In the earlier days of the Order, only Emerald Masks could become Dragons, with the belief that only someone who slayed a dragon could take that title for themselves. With the decrease of Dracotypes in the wild, and thus a decrease of Emerald Masks, rules have loosened. In Houses without Emerald Masks, the council will present a candidate to The Great House, who will approve or disprove of their selection. Once the role is granted, they are given their title.

The Dragon of the Great House is known as The Dragon King, and is the most respected person in the Order.
