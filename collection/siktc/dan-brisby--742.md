# Dan Brisby

**Dan Brisby** is a young boy from Archer's Peak, Wisconsin. A known bully, he blamed James for the death of his friend Robbie during the Archer's Peak incident.

## History

Upon spotting James at the memorial for the deceased children after the beginning of the Archer's Peak incident, Dan confronted him, claiming that he had gruesomely murdered all of the victims, and murdered Robbie for not liking him back. James shouts at him to "Shut the fuck up," causing him to be sent to the Principal's office.

## Physical appearance

James is a young Caucasian boy with blond hair.

## Personality

Dan is a known bully at Archer's Peak School, with even the faculty admitting that he needs comeuppance. He acts homophobic towards James, calling him a pervert.
