# Chimeratype

| “ | Simply put, they are the monsters from within. The man that becomes beast. When humankind's paranoia of each other manifests monsters in their place, most often in the shape of -- but not limited to -- animals. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Butcher |  |

**Chimeratypes** are a breed of monster fought by The Order of St. George. Coming from mankind's fear of becoming a beast, they are most commonly seen as werebeasts.

## History

While the full history of this monster is unknown, they have appeared through multiple centuries and cultures across the planet. They were originally classified as Mythotypes before their popularity shifted them into their own category.

## Description

A mix between the Animatype and the Duplicitype, the Chimera originates from fears of a man becoming a monster, of giving into animalistic origins. With the most common type being a werebeast, this creature is believed to have originated in Europe as the Werewolf. Due to the common folklore surrounding this creature, they are usually very easy to kill, often with silver bullets or blades. However, assumptions can be the death of a hunter, and a thorough investigation should be put into what created the beast before the hunt begins.

### Classifications

The most common Chimeratype is the Werewolf, usually in Western cultures. Other werebeasts can also be found, though, such as Kitsune, Were-Leopards, Were-Bears, and Were-Rats.

A second classification of Chimeratype is the **Chimeratype Deviant.** They are able to transform into any animal, human, or, conceivably, monster. While they are rare, they are incredibly dangerous due to their methods of camouflage. Hunters are expected to bait them, trap them, and burn them to ash.
