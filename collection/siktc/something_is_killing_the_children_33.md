 Something Is Killing the Children #33 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on September 20, 2023, by Boom! Studios . It is the thirty-third issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series.

 Synopsis 
 Help finally arrives in the town of Tribulation, but whose side are they on? Most importantly, with Cutter in deadly pursuit and the Duplicitype still very much a threat, what chance does Erica stand?
