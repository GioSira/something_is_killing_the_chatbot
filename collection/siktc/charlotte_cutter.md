 Charlotte Cutter was a highly-valued Black Mask hunter of the House of Cutter under The Order of St. George . A beloved member of her community, Charlotte was fierce, ruthless, and excessively violent, being trusted to kill anyone she was sent after, whether that be monsters, renegade hunters, or civilians. Despite her violent nature, or perhaps because of it, she was extremely immature in her interests, being extremely attached to her totem , Dolly , and murderous with anyone who came near her.
 In 2021, Charlotte was sent to assassinate Erica Slaughter , a rogue hunter in America. In order to draw Erica near, Charlotte killed Gary Slaughter and turned the Tribulation Duplicitype into a copy of her target . Erica would eventually kill Charlotte in battle, but not before Charlotte poisoned Gabi Lopez and ripped Erica's gold from her head, blinding her to the monsters in the real world.
