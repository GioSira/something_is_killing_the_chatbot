 Teddy is a Phantasmatype monster bound to The Old Dragon of the House of Slaughter . It is currently unknown what kind of monster Teddy was before being trapped and bound to his master, but he serves as a totem for him and sits on his desk. Teddy is described as "half-mad" and can often be heard eerily giggling.

 History 
 At some point, Teddy was bound to an initiate to the House of Slaughter who would later become The Old Dragon . He would sit on the Dragon's desk and on meeting tables. The Old Dragon describes him as a "real shit" who's gone half-mad.
