# Something Is Killing the Children (TV series)

***Something Is Killing the Children*** is an upcoming animated supernatural horror television series on Blumhouse based on the comic series of the same name from co-creators James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera. Initially in July of 2021, the series was put into development as a live-action adaptation with Trevor Macy and Mike Flanagan set to co-write and executive produce. However, creative differences resulted in the project being temporarily shelved. It was later reported that Netflix was developing a series but ultimately passed on it at the end of 2024 after opting not to move forward.
