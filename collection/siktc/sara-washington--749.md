# Sara Washington

**Sara Washington** was a young girl living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin in 2020. She was the first victim of the Oscuratype Monster in the Archer's Peak incident.

## History

Sara Washington had a criminal or suspicious uncle, who would later be blamed for her disappearance until it was shown there was no sign of her in his home.

Sara was told the story about James seeing a monster under the ravine; as she's noted to be one of Carly Monitz's friends, it's probable she was one of the first to hear the story as Tyler Monitz told it. She and the other girls spread around school the tale in order to discourage kids from going into the woods.

A month and a half before Erica Slaughter's arrival in Archer's Peak, Sara went missing about a half-mile from Lake Michigan. It was later determined that she was the first victim of the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother, which manifested due to the collective fear of the children. Eventually, her death would be blamed on Tommy Mahoney.
