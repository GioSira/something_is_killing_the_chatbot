 Gary Slaughter , nicknamed "Big Gary" , was an Azure Mask of the House of Slaughter under The Order of St. George . Serving as a hacker and farmer of training monsters , Gary was a close friend of Jessica and Cecilia Slaughter , the working partner of Buck Slaughter , and a beloved figure among many trainees of the House of Slaughter. After helping Erica Slaughter evade the House and continue to hunt monsters, he was interrogated and killed by Charlotte Cutter .
