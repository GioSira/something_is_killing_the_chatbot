# Kiersten White

**Kiersten White** is a 1 New York Times bestselling and Bram Stoker Award-winning author. She is co-writing *Something Is Killing the Children: Hope is a Knife* alongside James Tynion IV.

## Biography

White published her first book *Paranormalcy*, in 2010 with HarperCollins. Some of her other published works include *The House of Quiet*, the *And I Darken* trilogy, the *Sinister Summer* series, and the *Camelot Rising* trilogy.
