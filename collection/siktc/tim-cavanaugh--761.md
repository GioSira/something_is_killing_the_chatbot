# Tim Cavanaugh

> It's gonna fuck the kids up pretty good, though. Their brothers and sisters in chunks where they play Dodgeball. But hell, I guess we're plenty fucked up already.
>
> —Tim Cavanaugh , *Something Is Killing the Children* #6

**Tim Cavanaugh** is the coroner of Archer's Peak, Wisconsin who worked during the Archer's Peak incident of 2020. He is also the younger brother of Sheriff Joe Cavanaugh.

## Physical appearance

Tim is an older Caucasian man with brown hair and a wide, full beard.
