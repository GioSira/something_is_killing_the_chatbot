 Jantje Friese is a German co-showrunner, executive producer, and writer on the Netflix original supernatural drama, Something Is Killing the Children . 

 Biography 
 Friese and her husband, Baran bo Odar , received world-wide acclaim for creating the hit Netflix sci-fi drama Dark , which ran for three seasons before coming to a prearrange end. In the years that followed, the duo announced their next big production with Netflix, 1899 , a multilingual mystery thriller that was also planned to run for three seasons but was canceled prematurely by the streaming service.
 After renewing their first-look overall deal with Netflix, Friese and Odar were tapped to develop a series adaptation of the Something Is Killing the Children comic series.
