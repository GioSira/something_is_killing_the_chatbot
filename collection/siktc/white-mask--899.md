# White Mask

| “ | We are a faction of-- not a totem to-- the Order! Once in the Americas, I will rally a new team, selected by my hand from promising émigré, already proven eager to abandon their identities in favor of a paragon of tenacity. And they will be... will only ever have been White Masks. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — Father Alabaster Cutter , House of Slaughter #16 |  |

**White Masks** are a branch of The Order of St. George who don the color white and hunt monsters as a group. Often compared to packs of wolves, they are trained to take down large monsters as a team in order to minimize loss of life. They are usually known to have rivalries with the solo-hunting Black Masks, and in contrast to them are usually the largest branch of any given House. Despite this, they are often remarked to be cruel and callous.

The term "White Mask" is mainly used in the Americas, after Father Alabaster of the Cutters declared his settlement to be White Masks. In Europe, this branch is more often the Alabaster Masks, who are known for choosing promising members from the other houses and training them as expert hunters.

## Description

| “ | White Masks are typically the largest branch of each House, requiring the most recruits. They are pack hunters, trained to work together as a unit to bring down the largest monsters the Order faces in its work. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Slaughter |  |

The White Masks are the pack hunters of the Order. They are trained in multiple hunting techniques, with a specific focus on teamwork, strategy, and group attacks. With larger groups of hunters being used to take down larger monsters, they sometimes end up with a bit of an ego due to how impressive their kills can be; despite this, many often flounder when left alone. Because of this, many easily pass the Sacrament of the Hunt, as they are allowed to hunt in their usual team, but have a high mortality rate during the Blind Hunt, where they are expected to operate completely alone.

Group Hunters are expected to take down larger monsters on their own, while smaller monsters are taken down by Black or Silver Masks. The White Masks are often also used for larger cover-ups and assassinations, such as when large amounts of people witness a monster attack.

Despite being raised to be reliant on their team and in being the biggest branch of each House, White Masks are often socially aggressive, prideful, and violent. Most other branches have a dislike for them, which results in White Masks becoming more reliant on each other than other hunters.

The branch was originally born in the 1600s-1700s, when the colonization of America first began. Father Alabaster Cutter of the House of Cutter decided to emigrate to the colonies himself, rather than send members of his team, and argued that the Alabasters' way of fighting deserved its own identity, rather than being considered rejects from other houses.
