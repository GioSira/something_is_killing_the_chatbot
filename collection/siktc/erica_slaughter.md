 Erica Slaughter is a monster hunter who was taken in by the House of Slaughter at the age of twelve after her parents and friend were killed by an Oscuratype that would later become her totem , Octo . She was trained as a Black Mask by the Order of St. George to kill monsters in the field alone. At the end of the Game of Nowhere arc, she renounced all ties with the Order of St. George and began operating as a renegade, making her an enemy of the Order.

 Physical appearance 
 Erica is an attractive, athletic young adult woman with a muscular and toned yet slender physique. She has pale skin, various ear piercings, blond hair that she usually keeps tied up in a signature high ponytail, and piercing emerald green eyes. She often wears a black bandana-style mask that obscures the lower portion of her face. The black mask has a design with white teeth on it, with the four canines being upside down and extremely pronounced, denoting her status as a member of the House of Slaughter.
 Her faux driver's licenses in Archer's Peak and Tribulation list her height as 5'2" and 5'9", respectively.

 Powers and abilities 
 • Master Monster Hunter: Erica Slaughter is an incredibly skilled, nigh-unparalleled monster hunter. Erica was trained and mentored at a young age by her parental / older sister figure, Jessica Slaughter in monster hunting, and would ultimately grow up to become one of the House of Slaughter's most fearsome, resourceful and deadly hunters, skills she would retain even well-after her departure from the House of Slaughter. Specifically, Erica is a former member of the Black Masks, a faction of the House of Slaughter that specializes in solo hunting, with Erica being one of its foremost members, capable of killing several monsters on her own with ease. Even as a child at the the age of twelve, Erica was capable of critically injuring and almost killing an exceptionally powerful monster that had murdered her parents and best friend using a small kitchen knife (although Jessica Slaughter would assist her in slaying the creature for good.) Her skills have proven so formidable, that she was able to kill a fully grown Oscuratype monster by brutally dismembering it with her chainsaw after successfully tracking it down with relative ease, then later, single-handedly slaughter four of its young offspring after luring them into a trap, despite her left arm receiving prior injury by being bitten by the fifth, and most powerful monster among the offspring, (and while she did not manage to kill this one herself, she was still strong enough to fend it off long enough for her and her ally James to flee to safety,) all of which had already fed prior on several victims, growing substantially stronger. These feats highlight not only Erica's brutally efficient and nigh-unsurpassed skill, but also her cleverness and resourcefulness at monster hunting.
 • Peak Human Condition: After many years of hunting monsters and being put through the House of Slaughter's brutal training regime, Erica's body is in excellent physical form, near the upper limits of human capability.

 ° Peak Human Agility: Erica Slaughter is an exceptionally skilled acrobat whose ability to dodge bullets and evade the attacks of various monsters is unparalleled and almost superhuman. After tracking the Oscuratype mother to its den, she was able to effortlessly evade point-blank gunfire aimed at her from an enraged Tommy, who had mistaken her for his sister's killer.

 ° Peak Human Endurance
 • Indomitable Will: Erica has an unbreakable force of will even against seemingly impossible odds, making her an extremely formidable opponent. Erica has witnessed and endured many traumatic events throughout her life, (such as the deaths of her parents and several close friends,) and yet, remains committed to protecting children from the trauma she's experienced as a child, as well as battling the monsters that would seek to harm them. In Erica's own words, she once stated while speaking to the Dragon, (who in the aftermath of Archer's Peak, attempted to persuade her into returning back to the House of Slaughter,) "I'm going to keep doing what I do until it kills me, but I'm not going to do it for you." This sentence not only highlights Erica's unshakable commitment to protecting children from the horrors and trauma she's faced as a child, but also her own autonomy and self-agency, as she is no longer under any obligation to do so, but willingly chooses to despite the consequences weighing heavily on her at times. For example, her separation from the House of Slaughter also thereby made them her enemy, and this excludes the fact monster hunting was always highly dangerous, even for the most seasoned and experienced hunters/huntresses. Nevertheless, she remains steadfast and firm despite this and the countless other trials she's faced, further cementing Erica Slaughter's willpower and resolve.

• Skilled Tactician and Strategist 
 • Master Acrobat: Erica is an exceptional natural athlete and elite-level gymnast and acrobat, possessing a peak human level of agility and acrobatic skill. She is capable of performing impeccably precise acrobatic maneuvers in combat, as well as dodging a flurry of attacks from several monsters, as seen during her battle with the four Oscuratypes. 
 • Master Martial Artist: Erica Slaughter is a highly gifted and formidable hand-to-hand combatant. Still a child, Erica was taught how to fight by her mentor, Jessica Slaughter, who would help mold her into an exceptional martial artist. Erica employs a brutal but methodical fighting style, as seen when she was able to single-handedly defeat four young Oscuratype monsters, easily breaking one of their legs with a single punch during combat. She also effortlessly broke Tommy's left arm in self-defense after he attempted to grab her and knocked out Aaron (a fellow monster hunter,) with a single blow to the head.
 • Medical Knowledge: Erica clearly posseses some degree of medical knowledge, and is capable of administering first aid, even with improvised and or limited medical supplies, as seen when she treats the wounds she received on her left arm after being bitten by a monster by stitching them herself at John's house.
 • Weapons Proficiency: Erica Slaughter is highly proficient with a wide variety of weapons; everything from combat knives and handguns, to improvised and makeshift weaponry such as machetes. Notably, Erica is exceptionally creative and resourceful in combat. Her ingenuity allows her to turn virtually any common object into a deadly weapon, capable of killing or incapacitating the enemy. A best example of this is when she takes James out shopping at a House Depot, where she stocks up on a plethora of different items she would later use to hunt down and kill the Oscuratype mother in Archer's Peak, including the chainsaw she would later use to dismember it.
