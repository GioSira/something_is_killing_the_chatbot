 Buck Slaughter (current aliases unknown) is the Head of the Azure Masks of the House of Slaughter under The Order of St. George . He was formerly the working partner of Gary Slaughter at the Farm around the year 2000, before moving up in the ranks enough that by 2020 he was the leader of his branch of the house. He is known to rarely speak, and as a totem of a golden bull plush toy.

 Physical appearance 
 History 
 Buck was the child of a cello or bass player in the Kansas City Orchestra, and received a toy golden bull to match its symbol. His family originates from the Alabaster Masks of the New York House , along with Gerde Slaughter . For a time, he worked with Gary Slaughter on the Farm , raising monsters and working espionage for the House of Slaughter . 
 By the year 2020, Buck had ascended the ranks to become the head of the Azure Masks of the House of Slaughter.
