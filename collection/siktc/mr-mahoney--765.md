# Mr. Mahoney

**Mr. Mahoney** is the father of the late Sophie Mahoney and of Tommy Mahoney, who took the fall for the murders in order to protect the rest of the town.

## Physical appearance

Mr. Mahoney is an older, out of shape Caucasian man with brown hair, though he is balding in the center of his head.
