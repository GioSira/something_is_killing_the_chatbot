 The unnamed mother and father of the Parker family lived in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the 2020 Archer's Peak incident . One of their children would be killed by the Oscuratype Mother , and while at the school gymnasium to identify the body, their other three children would be murdered by its litter .

 History 
 The Parkers were a couple who had four children: Corey , Brendan , and Jason , along with a fourth unnamed child. Their fourth child would be attacked and killed by the Oscuratype Mother in 2020, with their body later being found in its den by the lake. 
 The parents were called to come to the school gymnasium, the only place big enough to hold all the body parts, and identify their child's remains. At Jason's request, the other boys came along, though Jason would later run into the woods, followed by his brothers. The Parker parents attempted to "barge in" to the gym to see their child, but Tim Cavanaugh stopped them, telling them to keep things orderly. They would still be arguing with him and sobbing when Tommy Mahoney arrived. 
 Sheriff Joe Cavanaugh would find Tommy among the remains of their other boys, and expressed horror at the thought of having to tell them that they'd lost three more of their children. They were still most likely present at the gymnasium when the Oscuratype Children would attack and kill another girl in the parking lot. Their children's deaths would be blamed on Tommy Mahoney. 

 Physical appearances 
 The Parkers are only briefly seen; Ms. Parker has short brown hair, matching her son, Brendan , while Mr. Parker has dirty blonde hair and a beard. Both appear to be of European descent.
