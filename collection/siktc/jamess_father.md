 James' father lives in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin with his son , whom he struggled to protect his son during the Archer's Peak incident .

 History 
 James's Father was the parent to an unnamed daughter and James. He and the children's mother were separated, with the children living with him in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin . He is often busy at work or taking James's sister to sporting events. 
 While James had a sleepover with his friends, James's father slept upstairs until hearing loud noises outside. He went out to scold the boys for making noise, only to find a bloody James surrounded by the torn-apart bodies of his friends. Terrified, James's father hugged him and asked what had done this; a tearful James said it was him. 
 James would later be questioned by police, but let go as the attack was thought to be from an animal. The family would temporarily move into a hotel to avoid media attention, but James's father would have to work long hours, often leaving James alone. He did call his son every day to ensure his safety, however. 
 After the arrest of Tommy Mahoney , James would stay with his mother in Milwaukee for a while. 

 Physical appearance 
 James's father is only briefly seen. He has the same black hair and brown eyes as his son, and a similar Caucasian skintone. His hairline is receding, and he is drawn with arm and leg hair. 

 Personality 
 Little is known about James's father. He seems to care deeply for his son, running to him when he's in danger and often checking up on him after the traumatic event. He moved the family into a hotel room in order to avoid press following the deaths of James's friends, and called James often when walking home from school. He is often working and seems well-off financially, as they have a large home. 
 He often calls James by a nickname, either "Jimmy" or "Jamie."
