# Sara

> There aren't going to be any more dead kids. She promised that, too.
>
> —Sara , *Something Is Killing the Children* #0

**Sara** is a young girl who survived an Oscuratype monster attack sometime before the Archer's Peak incident. Losing one arm, one eye and both legs to an Oscuratype that killed her friends, Sara escaped the hospital in order to help Erica Slaughter kill the beast. Though she lived somewhat normally afterward, she eventually kidnapped by The Great House of St. George for recruitment.

## History

Sara lived with both her parents in an unnamed town. She and four of her friends were attacked by an Oscuratype monster, leaving her the only survivor. Her injuries also caused her to lose her left arm, left eye, and both legs. While in the hospital, she was approached by Erica Slaughter, who assured her that she had not hallucinated the monster, and that she was a monster-hunter here to kill the creature. Sara agreed to escape the hospital with her, despite knowing it would upset her parents, and camped with her in the woods for several days while trying to draw the creature out. She overheard Erica argue with someone on the phone and Octo, then hugged her when Erica said she was often trying to help children.

Not wanting Sara to be in danger, Erica left her in a wagon by the side of the road. Waiting for a long while, Sara eventually heard Erica fight and kill the monster, before returning covered in blood. Erica answered a phone call from her base, telling her to go to Archer's Peak next, and asked Sara if she could use her shower. Knowing her Mom's schedule, Sara knew the house would be empty and allowed this.

Following Erica's departure, Sara continued to live with her parents, who remained upset at her for leaving the hospital. Eventually, her mother was approached by a man claiming to be a policeman, later revealed to be Victor Saint-Georges, who asked to interview Sara at an ice cream parlor. While Sara was under the impression her parents were in the parking lot, she later realized they hadn't been there a long while; their location and status is currently unknown. After Victor revealed he knew about the monsters and asked her about Erica, Sara believed that he was trying to harm her and set off to warn her. However, without her parents nearby and having been drugged by the ice cream, Sara collapsed on the ground. Victor then seemingly recruited her into The Great House of St. George; it is unknown at this time whether she survived The Sacrament of Initiation.

## Physical appearance

Sara is a young girl of European descent, with white skin and short brunette hair with bangs and green eyes. She often wears a black headband in her hair and a pink jacket, tied over the arm she lost. Her left arm and both legs had to be amputated following a monster attack, and she wears a white patch over her left eye.

## Personality

Following her attack, Sara is very quiet and suspicious. She is usually a rule-follower, not wanting to eat ice cream before dinner and keeping Erica's secret until meeting another monster-hunter. However, she will break rules and disappoint authority for the greater good, such as when she escaped the hospital with Erica. Despite being in danger, she had full trust in Erica to protect her and later allowed her to use her shower before departing town. She is deeply loyal to Erica, who she knows is helping other victims of monster attacks, and refuses to divulge information that could harm her.
