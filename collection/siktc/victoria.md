 Victoria is an unseen woman who lived in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the 2020 Archer's Peak incident . Her daughter, Sally , was among the missing children who were later found as corpses in the monster 's lair.

 History 
 Victoria was the mother of a girl named Sally . Sally would go missing during the Archer's Peak incident , along with multiple other children. When several body parts were found in a den near the lake, parents were called to come to the school gymnasium, the only place big enough to hold all the corpses, to identify the bodies. Victoria would call Ms. Mahoney to tell her where to go. 
 It is currently unknown if she was among the crowd that remained at the gym when the Oscuratypes attacked.
