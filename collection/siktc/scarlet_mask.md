# Scarlet Mask

The Scarlet Mask of the North American Houses. 
 Scarlet Masks are a branch of The Order of St. George who don the color red and serve as book- and housekeepers of their specified House. Extremely important to the Order as their record-keepers, they are also sometimes looked down upon by Hunters as simply servants. However, they help all Houses run smoothly, keep track of every Hunter, and catalog information for future reference.

 Description 

 “

 Scarlet Masks are the keepers of their House and its records. They make sure the House is kept in pristine condition, feeding its hunters, washing their clothes, cleaning their rooms. They also oversee the library of each House, recording each hunter's missions, and documenting the different classes of monsters the hunters face in their work, so that the House can learn from its past mistakes and rise above them into the future. They write in the blood of monsters so that only members of the Order can read their work. The majority of those who decide not to participate in the Blind Hunt become Scarlet Masks. 

 ”

 — The Book of Slaughter 

 The Scarlet Masks are not active hunters within the Order of St. George. While initially trained as White or Black Masks, many apprentices either feel their calling within the record-keeping or housekeeping aspects of the Scarlet life, or else find themselves unlikely to survive the Blind Hunt and wish for some other way to be useful without field work. There are multiple Scarlet Masks within each House, in order to keep it all in proper order so that the Hunters don't have to worry about their base of operations. 
 The main job of the Scarlets is to catalog information within the Order. They will record every hunter's missions, life, and death; as well as every monster sighting, behavior pattern, and death. They oversee the library, usually keeping books written only in monster-blood so only those of the Order can read their contents. In short, the members of this branch are usually organizational, ordered, and perceptive.
 Often, Hunters look down upon Scarlet Masks as unskilled laborers; this isn't helped by the fact the Scarlets also take care of each House, and thus act as mealmakers and cleaners for the other Masks, which give them the perception of servants. This can also cause Scarlets to consider other Masks to be rude, thoughtless, and stupid. The Scarlets are not to be underestimated, however, as they hold knowledge of every aspect of the House, a large amount of the Order's history, and secrets many other Masks will never be privy to.
