# John Richards

> I need to understand what's happening here, because none of this makes any fucking sense to me. I feel like I'm suffocating and I want to scream but I don't want to freak out the kid, but the kids have already seen things more horrible than I can imagine. Anything I do feels like it's not enough. All I want to do is keep them safe.
>
> —Officer John Richards , *Something Is Killing the Children* #12

**John Richards**, occasionally referred to as **Tom Richards**, was a police officer in the town of Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the Archer's Peak incident of 2020. He was known to interview victims, survivors, and families of missing children. Told to follow a suspicious Erica Slaughter, John became wrapped up in the monster hunt going on around him and appointed himself the protector of Bian. Unfortunately, after being told the truth about monsters, he was killed by Cecilia Slaughter.

## Physical appearance

Officer Richards was an older Caucasian man with dark brown hair.
