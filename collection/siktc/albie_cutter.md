 Albert "Albie" Cutter is a Scarlet Mask positioned in the House of Cutter under The Order of St. George . As the keeper of the house, he had an intimate knowledge of the secrets of both its residents and guests. Following the death of his charge, Charlotte Cutter , he became bitter towards the House of Slaughter , who had requested her assistance in the failed assassination of Erica Slaughter .

 History 
 Past 
 At some point, Albie was recruited into The Order of St. George under the House of Cutter . He became a Scarlet Mask , and was the keeper of the entire House, learning quickly how to discern secrets and plans from its members. He also raised Charlotte Cutter from childhood into her position as a Black Mask , and she was considered his "special favorite." 

 Tribulation Incident and Aftermath 
 In 2021, Albie sent Charlotte word that she had been assigned to kill a renegade hunter in America. However, Charlotte was killed by Erica Slaughter , giving Albie a slight grudge against the House that had sent her to her death. It is unknown if he knows specifically of Cecilia Slaughter 's involvement in Charlotte's death, but he had a specific distaste for her as well.
 When Maxine Slaughter trained in the House to become Slaughter's only Black Mask, Albie conversed with her, having found it interesting how interested she was in reading. When she admitted she knew she was a pawn but wanted to know more about the game she was being played in, Albie told her where to sit in order to eavesdrop on important meetings, such as the one between Cecilia and Victor Saint-Georges that would happen soon. After chaperoning both guests to their place, he spotted Maxine in the spot, and said nothing before leaving.
