 Thomas "Tommy" Mahoney was a manager at Applebeans who became invested in the disappearance of his younger sister, Sophie , and began to suspect newcomer Erica Slaughter after she acted strange in his restaurant. He would steal his father's gun and follow her to a monster 's den, where she would forcibly stab him with horde gold so that he could see the danger he was in and escape with James and Bian . A confused Tommy would then see the monster's children in the school parking lot, where several mourners had gathered, and act as a lookout for the Sheriff as everyone barricaded themselves into the gym. When the White Masks of the House of Slaughter arrived to cover up the incident, Tommy took the blame for all of the murders in order to prevent them from killing everyone in the school. This plan worked, and Tommy was arrested and charged with the deaths of multiple children.
