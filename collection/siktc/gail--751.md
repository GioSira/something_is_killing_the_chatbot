# Gail

> I was there when my father passed, and it wasn't scary. It was peaceful. He had been in a lot of pain, and then he was at rest. And ever since then, I've known he's been in Heaven looking down on me.
>
> —Gail , *Something Is Killing the Children* #8

**Gail** is an elderly woman who worked as a secretary and receptionist at the Archer's Peak police department, and later became the adoptive mother of Bian following the Archer's Peak incident of 2020.

## History

Gail worked at the Archer's Peak Police Department as a receptionist or secretary. She had a partner named David who used to keep her awake snoring until he got a CPAP; she would use cremes to help her sleep. At some point, she was present for the death of her father. As he had been in a lot of pain, she saw his death as peaceful and a way for him to rest.

During the Archer's Peak incident, Gail was put in charge of watching Bian while the police investigated and identified the bodies she was found with. Gail was very sweet and kindly to her, though slightly thrown by her morbidity. She offered Erica Slaughter coffee and allowed her to say, as Bian knew and recognized her. When Erica returned with Aaron Slaughter and Officer Richards threatened them, Gail attempted to call the Sheriff, only for Aaron to knock her out.

Gail would later take in Bian after the girl's family failed to pick her up.

## Physical appearance

Gail is an older Caucasian woman with white hair.

## Personality

Gail is very sweet, kindly and patient, but does not take nonsense lightly. She is unafraid of death, but even still is thrown by Bian's more direct questions about it. She also appears to be religious.
