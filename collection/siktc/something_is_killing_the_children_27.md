 Something Is Killing the Children #27 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on December 21, 2022 by Boom! Studios . It is the twenty-seventh issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series.

 Synopsis 
 Leaving death and tragedy in her wake, a grief-burdened Erica hunts for the terrifying Duplicitype. With nothing left to lose and seemingly no one in her corner, Erica is faced with a choice to reach out to an unexpected source of help, or find herself dealing with both a monster and her human enemies alone...
