 Something Is Killing the Children #24 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on June 22, 2022 by Boom! Studios . It is the twenty-fourth issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series.

 Synopsis 
 While Cutter's machinations continue to unfold at the House of Slaughter , Erica has tracked a terrifying new enemy within a small town in New Mexico. With her past quickly catching up with her, Erica must don the mask once again and head into the heart of the danger if she wants any hope of stopping this monstrous entity.
