# Fall of the House of Slaughter (comic series)

***Fall of the House of Slaughter*** is an upcoming four-issue mini-series horror comic book created by James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera, who wrote and illustrated the series respectively. It is a spinoff of the *Something Is Killing the Children* comic series, existing within the Slaughterverse, and therefore, all issues are published by Boom! Studios.

## Synopsis

The world of *Something Is Killing the Children* is about to change. Forever.

Writer Tate Brombal (*House of Slaughter*) and artist Adriano Turtulici (*You'll Do Bad Things*) usher in a new era of James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera's SlaughterVerse as the House of Slaughter is plunged into turmoil in *FALL OF THE HOUSE OF SLAUGHTER*.

As the House of Slaughter's Old Dragon enters his final days, Cecilia Slaughter and the other Heads gather to determine his successor.

Deadly ambitions begin to take shape as the House and Order prepare for a new legacy to begin—and the conflict to determine that new legacy will be lethal.

Can the House of Slaughter survive a new Dragon coming into power? Find out in this all-new mini-series!
