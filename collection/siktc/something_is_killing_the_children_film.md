 Something Is Killing the Children is an upcoming live-action supernatural horror film produced by Blumhouse based on the comic series of the same name from co-creators James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera . The film will be produced by Tynion and Stephen Christy, and directed by Craig Zobel .

 Production 
 On July 23, 2026, during a signing and Q&A on Trinity Comics’ Whatnot stream, James Tynion IV revealed that there is a completed screenplay for the live-action film, but the delay is due to the companion animated series . The creative team wants to complete the "series bible" for the animated series before taking both adaptations out to studios and streamers simultaneously in hopes of finding a partner interested in both projects, though Tynion noted that if a buyer only wants the live-action film, the animated series can still be shopped elsewhere.
