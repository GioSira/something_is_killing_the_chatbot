# Blumhouse

**Blumhouse Productions, LLC,** is an American independent television and film production company founded by Jason Blum and Amy Israel in 2000. Through a ten-year first-look deal in 2014, many of their theatrically released films are owned and distributed by Universal Pictures.

The company is now home to both a live-action film and animated television adaptation for the ongoing horror comic book *Something Is Killing the Children*, created by James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera, who wrote and illustrated the series respectively. All issues are published by Boom! Studios.
