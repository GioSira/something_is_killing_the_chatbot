# House of Slaughter 1

**House of Slaughter #1** is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on October 27, 2021 by Boom! Studios. It is the first issue in the *House of Slaughter* comic series.

## Synopsis

Discover the inner workings of the House of Slaughter in this new horror series exploring the secret history of the Order that forged Erica Slaughter into the monster hunter she is today.

- Aaron Slaughter
