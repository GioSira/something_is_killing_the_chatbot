# Joe Cavanaugh

> This is all out of my depth. This is all so fucking horrible I don't know how to process any of it. And the next part is going to be so much worse.
>
> —Sheriff Joe Cavanaugh , *Something Is Killing the Children* #6

**Joe Cavanaugh** is the Sheriff of Archer's Peak, Wisconsin and the presiding officer over the Archer's Peak incident of 2020. Intent on solving the disappearances and deaths of multiple children, Joe ended up roped into Erica Slaughter's hunt and only barely escaped with his life after the House of Slaughter intervened. He was forced to arrest Tommy Mahoney for the murders, despite knowing his innocence, in order to save the rest of the town.

## Physical appearance

Sheriff Cavanaugh is an older Caucasian man with black hair, brown eyes, and a mustache.
