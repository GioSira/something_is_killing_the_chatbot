# Jessica Slaughter

> These kids, they've watched their friends get killed and eaten by something unthinkable. They see me show up and they think they've been saved. The look in their eyes, that's what kills me every time. And the way it changes when they realize...
>
> —Jessica Slaughter , *Something Is Killing the Children* #20

**Jessica Slaughter** was the Head of the Black Masks of the House of Slaughter, under The Order of St. George. The trainer of Aaron and Erica Slaughter, Jessica broke order rules in recruiting the latter and harshly defended both of her apprentices throughout their training. The horrors she had seen during her hunting career left her bitter and jaded towards the world, and she often coped through an alcohol addiction which would cause her to break down in front of her apprentices. She was killed in a hunt that The Old Dragon sent her on, with Erica present.

## Physical appearance

Jessica was a young Caucasian woman with a fit physique and short blonde hair.
