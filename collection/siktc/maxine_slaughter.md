 Maxine Slaughter is a Black Mask monster hunter of the House of Slaughter of The Order of St. George . Originally a White-Masked pack hunter, Maxine would take on the mantle of the House's only remaining Black Mask after the death of Aaron Slaughter and departure of Erica Slaughter . To achieve this end, she would train first under the House of Boucher in New Orleans, and then the House of Cutter in England. Deeply loyal to Cecilia Slaughter , Maxine was told in her training to question the authorities she'd been taught all her life to defer to. 

 Physical appearance 
 Maxine is a woman of African descent with pink eyes and short, dark hair, styled to favor a Mohawk.
