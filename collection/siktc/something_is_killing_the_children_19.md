 Something Is Killing the Children #19 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on August 25, 2021 by Boom! Studios . It is the nineteenth issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series.

 Synopsis 
 Erica must play the Game of Nowhere, a secretive binding ritual of the Order of St. George , but not everyone wants her to succeed.

 Plot 
 Aaron Slaughter is awoken by Tybalt , who threatens him with a knife, along with his twin brother Paris . Despite Aaron's short attempt to grab the knife, the twins get it back and pin him to the bed, calling him a runt and mocking him for crying. They then mock him for almost having a sister who was sent to die on Big Gary 's farm. Aaron angrily shouts that she is going to live, and that she'd almost killed an Oscuratype the day before. Cecilia then enters and sends the twins off, before having Aaron get dressed to leave. 
 In Erica's trance, she relives the moment she decided to fight back against the Oscuratype. She grabs a kitchen knife and attacks it while it eats her father, stabbing it in the eye. She then hears her mother beg her to go to the neighbors and get them to call the police. Erica runs, but stops once outside the house, remembering Aaron and Jessica 's warnings. Erica then turns, speaking directly to the monster and saying that it's trying to trick her, which the ghostly octopus spectre affirms. While it tries to make her leave, saying that the memory will still hurt, Erica knows that any other option wouldn't be real, and it's trying to take away the memory of it losing. She goes inside, accepting that her parents are dead and refusing to listen to the monster's manipulations. 
 She lays back down under the monster, and narrates Jessica entering the house. As the monster saw her in surprise, Erica stabbed it in the neck, and was pushed aside as Jessica fought the creature. Finally feeling safe, Erica hugged Octo, waiting for it to be over, only for Jessica to ask if the plush is hers and if she loves it. She told Erica to hand it to her, and then she can end this. Erica hugged Octo, gave him a kiss goodbye, and let him go. Speaking another language, Jessica then bound Octo into the plush. Now sitting in the white void of the Game of Nowhere , Erica asks the monster if it feels sorry for killing her family. It says no, and she says that it's time to wake up. 
 Sitting up in the barn, Octo tells Erica that the others thought she was going to die, so they left her. Leaving the barn to see one of Gary's dogs, she responds, "Yeah, well... I didn't. So what's next?"
