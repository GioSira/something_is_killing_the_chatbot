 Something Is Killing the Children #36 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on April 24, 2024, by Boom! Studios . It is the thirty-sixth issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series, and the first issue of the five-part Road Stories arc.

 Synopsis 
 Something is Killing the Children returns to reveal more of Erica Slaughter 's formative past, set before the Archer's Peak saga.
