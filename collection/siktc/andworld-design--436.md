# AndWorld Design

**AndWorld Design** is a lettering studio that handles the lettering on the *Something Is Killing the Children* comic series, as well as its Slaughterverse spinoffs.

## Studio members

- Deron Bennett: Owner
- Kat Vendetti: Editor
- Marlon B.: Social Media Design Coordinator
- Morgan Martinez: Multi-disciplinary Art Director, Data Visualization Analyst
- DC Hopkins: Letterer and Designer
