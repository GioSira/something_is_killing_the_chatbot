 Noah was a young boy living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin in 2020. A friend of James , Karl and Tyler Monitz , Noah was killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother while at a sleepover with his friends.

 History 
 Noah was a friend of James and Tyler Monitz ; he and the latter encouraged James to ask out his crush, Karl , who later joined their friend group. At one sleepover, the boys were caught trying to sneak out. 
 At another sleepover, Noah was dared to run around the house naked. Following, Tyler prompted James to talk about the monster he had seen in the ravine. Noah calls bullshit on his story, so James dares the boys to all go to the bridge and check. Noah angrily does so, upset that they have to walk all the way down there to look for his "made-up monster." He shouts that he is brave, only to see an Oscuratype monster approaching. James is separated from the group; when he returns, Noah is barely alive, and has been cut in half by the creature. He tells James that he can't feel his legs, and begs for him to take him with him, before dying from his injuries.
 While the deaths would be blamed on a bear originally, people suspected James of killing his friends for a while. A memorial was placed up for Noah and the other boys at Archer's Peak High School . Eventually, his death would be blamed on Tommy Mahoney .
