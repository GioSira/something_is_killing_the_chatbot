# Miquel Muerto

**Miquel Muerto** is a Spanish comic book colorist for the *Something Is Killing the Children* comic series and its Slaughterverse spinoffs.

## Biography

Miquel Muerto studied graphic design first and illustration late, both at EASD Escola Superior de Disseny i Art "Llotja”. Afterward, he started running a small press with friends doing zines while working as a graphic designer and illustrator for a few years. *La Senda de los Druidas* (Sallybooks, 2016) was his comic artist debut.
