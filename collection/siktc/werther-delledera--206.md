# Werther Dell'Edera

**Werther Dell'Edera** is an Italian comic book artist and co-creator of the *Something Is Killing the Children* comic series, as well as its Slaughterverse spinoffs. He is also an executive producer on the Netflix original *supernatural drama* of the same name.

## Biography

Dell'Edera is an award-winning artist born in south Italy, who has worked with some of the biggest publishers in both Italy and America. In addition to co-creating and illustrating the *Something Is Killing the Children* comic series, for which he was nominated for an Eisner award in 2020, Dell'Edera's work in Italy on *Dylan Dog* and *The Crow: Memnto Mori* earned him awards for Best Cover Artist, Best Series and Best Artist.

Dell'Edera has also worked on the *Spider-Man: Family Business* graphic novel and has provided interior art for the unreleased *Aliens: Colonial Marines - Rising Threat* comic for Dark Horse. In 2021, he and James Tynion IV announced a spinoff comic to *Something is Killing the Children*, titled *House of Slaughter*.
