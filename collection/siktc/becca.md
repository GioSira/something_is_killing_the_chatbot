 Becca was a young girl who lived nearby and was best friends with a girl named Erica . During a sleepover c. 2000, Becca was killed by a vicious Oscuratype monster which would later be bound to Erica as Octo .

 History 
 Little is known about Becca, due to how little Erica Slaughter talks about her. She was a girl about Erica's age who was her best friend during her pre- Order life. The two girls were having a sleepover while Erica's parents went out to get them pizza, leaving them alone. An Oscuratype monster would then attack the two, chasing them upstairs. Becca tripped and fell, giving the monster the time to catch up to her and cut her in half, before eating her. 
 Her death would be covered up by The Order of St. George , and the monster that killed her bound to Octo , Erica's Totem .
