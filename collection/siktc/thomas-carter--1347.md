# Thomas Carter

**Sheriff Thomas Carter** is a man who was the sheriff of Tribulation, New Mexico during the 2021 Duplicitype Incident. Left in the dark about the existence of monsters, Carter was led by Charlotte Cutter to believe that Erica Slaughter was behind the murders, and allowed her to hunt the woman across his town. Following the death of Cutter and apparent death of Erica, he was paid off by Cecilia Slaughter despite his confusion and insistence for answers.

## Physical appearance

Thomas Carter is a middle-aged man of European descent with light brown hair. and dark brown eyes. He is usually wearing a cowboy hat.
