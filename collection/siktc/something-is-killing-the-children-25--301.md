# Something Is Killing the Children 25

***Something Is Killing the Children* #25** is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on July 27, 2022 by Boom! Studios. It is the twenty-fifth issue in the *Something Is Killing the Children* comic series.

## Synopsis

While Erica's blades are occupied with an entity unlike anything they've faced, she receives a phone call... with tragedy on the other end of the line. As things look dire, someone Erica once saved may have to find unexpected help to return the favor...

- Erica Slaughter
