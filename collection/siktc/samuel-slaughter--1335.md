# Samuel Slaughter

> Perhaps it time[sic] I found a House of mine own. Without Cutter. Without Carnage. Formed of my fellow travellerrs and the natives, if they shall have me. What foundation this would be, rooted within a land and people that understand my survival? Borne of those we lost. A House that was founded in a great and terrible Slaughter.
>
> —Samuel Cutter , *Book of Cutter*

**Samuel Slaughter,** formerly known as **Samuel Cutter**, was the founder of the House of Slaughter, an American branch of The Order of St. George. Originally a Scarlet Mask in London, England, he was sent to the American colonies with Brutus the Black in order to found a house within New York. However, upon the death of his colleagues and the discovery of the indigenous genocide taking place under England, Samuel fled to the Chigagon area, where he would found the House of Slaughter with other pioneers and native peoples.

## History

Samuel Cutter was born c. 1635. He was a street urchin in London as a child, before being found and recruited by the House of Cutter; previous to the colonization of America, he had never left London. He was trained and eventually became a Scarlet Mask.

On June 6, 1666, after a day of spreading a story to the local newspaper to cover up Brutus Cutter's bloody attack on a Thames serpent, Samuel was called to a rare communion with the House's Dragon. The Dragon told him that as the most devoted Scarlet Mask, he was to be sent to the American colonies with Brutus in order to create a new house. Though unhappy with the assignment, Samuel did indeed board the ship the next day, under disguise as a Catholic priest, though he was almost late to the early departure and soon became seasick. The sailors asked the faux priest to lead Mass on Sundays, which caused Brutus to laugh.

Samuel would attend to the sick and hungry on the ship, help cook and clean, as well as report confessions to Brutus. Upon a stowaway child being discovered, he would convince the sailors not to throw him into the sea, before taking the child in as a possible ward, being reminded of himself as an urchin. However, the boy would listen in fear to the sailors' tales of sea serpents, which they insisted were true. His fear, combined with the fears of many previous seagoing children, would manifest a real serpent that would attack the ship on the night of July 2. In order to keep the ship on its course, Brutus sacrificed the boy to the serpent, which then left the rest of them alone. Samuel cried for the child, and was unable to look at Brutus afterwards.

The men would arrive in what would become New York on July 28, slightly starved and sickly. Samuel was entranced by the large forests, and the strange symbols left by the native peoples, though he himself feared to go into the woods. Brutus kept Samuel close to him in order to survey the land and report back to Cutter if they had found a suitable place for their new House. A little over a week later, after exploring the town for some time, several Frenchmen from a northern colony arrived on promises of trade. Meeting with two hunters from the party, Julien and François Carnage, Samuel was entranced by the Black-Masked Julien, and would meet with him nightly to compare intelligence and culture. Julien would also begin to train Samuel in swordplay, while Samuel would give him lessons in more Scarlet-style work.

Brutus and Samuel would spend three days within the woods, searching for monsters. Despite Samuel's trepidation, Brutus would insist they enter a strange cave, to which they would find countless bones and desecrated bodies of indigenous children. Upon finding no monsters nearby, Brutus revealed to a horrified Samuel that the English had been the ones to do this, making him realize that the symbols left by the natives were there to warn each other of the settlers. François and Julien then arrived, claiming they were also searching for monsters, but Julien then swiftly cut off Brutus's arm and attacked Samuel, declaring that Carnage must take these lands before any other. Still alive, Brutus decapitated Julien and clubbed François to death, before dying in Samuel's arms. Samuel would then take Brutus's Black Mask as his own.

Samuel sent word back to the House of Cutter of these events, but after everything that had happened, Samuel refused to stay in New York. He eventually abandoned post, following native trails and trade routes and meeting with both those nations and other settlers. He and they would teach each other their ways, until he decided to settle by the Great Lake of the Chicagon area. Though doubting his mission and allegiance to those who had begun a genocide, he would decide to found the House of Slaughter with the other travelers and natives of the area, cutting themselves off from the European houses and remembering the violence of the past.

He would stay within the House of Slaughter for the rest of his life, refusing to speak to the House of Cutter ever again. Though this house raised concerns with their superiors, Samuel still remained loyal to the Order at large, and so the Great House would allow him to continue to do what he saw fit. In response, Cutter would funnel resources into the New York post he abandoned, which would become the House of Blood.

## Personality

Samuel was very contemplative, and usually pretty wordy and well-spoken. He disliked violence, especially that which is senseless, and had a large sense of empathy for others that is rare in the Order. However, he would also be snarky and blunt with those he disliked. For a time, he believed that the Order should be loyal to each other regardless of House or nationality, but following the betrayal of Julien, he lost trust in the European Houses. He says that he holds a sorrow "borne of monstrum and things survived."

He liked to write and paint, and would be calmed by sunsets.

## Physical appearance

Samuel was thirty-one when he arrived in America in 1666, and was of English descent. His illustration of himself shows him with receding dark hair.
