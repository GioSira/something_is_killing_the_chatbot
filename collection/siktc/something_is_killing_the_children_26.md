 Something Is Killing the Children #26 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on November 16, 2022 by Boom! Studios . It is the twenty-sixth issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series.

 Synopsis 
 With the last person in her corner gone and the Order of St. George closing in, Erica finds herself totally alone and without a prayer. Can she take on the terrifying Doppelganger Duplicitype by herself, or is this the end of her story?
