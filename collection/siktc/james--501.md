# James

**James** is a young boy from Archer's Peak, Wisconsin, who was a survivor of an Oscuratype attack. The only one to escape from his sleepover group, James was traumatized by the death of his friends by a monster he had thought he'd made up. Upon Erica Slaughter's arrival, James began to assist her in tracking down and killing the Oscuratype Monster and its children.

## History

### Early life

James's parents were divorced, and he primarily lived with his father and sister in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin. His father is often at field hockey events with his sister.

James is gay, and faces homophobia from several classmates, notably Dan Brisby, who calls him a "pervert." James also had a major crush on Karl, which he talked about to his friends Noah and Tyler Monitz at sleepovers; this was a recent development, as he hadn't had "sleepover friends" for very long. His friends encouraged James to ask him out, before they attempted to sneak out of the house. They were caught and got in trouble for this. When James asked Karl out, Karl told him that they should remain platonic. However, he did join the friend group.

### Archer's Peak incident

While alone in a dark room of the house, James imagined a creepy face with long arms outside the window, and scared himself so much he ran to his room. He then told Tyler at their next sleepover, and the story spread far enough to create an Oscuratype monster.

At a sleepover with Noah, Tyler and Karl, James chooses "truth" in a game of Truth or Dare. He tells a story about hearing a noise in the ravine while home alone, and turning out the lights to look outside, before describing a long-fingered monster emerging from the woods. Noah disbelieves him, so James dares him to go look for himself. All four boys snuck out of the house to check under the bridge for the monster. Noah spots it first, just as James trips and falls down the hill. He hears his friends scream. He runs up the hill to see the monster violently kill his friends. Before it kills him, however, his father arrives, causing it to leave. Terrified, James cries that this was his fault.

James was soon picked up by the police, where he recounted the incident, but did not describe the monster, fearing he wouldn't be believed. Due to the media attention, James and his family temporarily moved to a hotel room.

Two weeks following the murders, James was confronted by Dan at a memorial set up for the missing children outside the school. Dan accused him of doing the murders, causing James to shout at him to "shut the fuck up." He was sent to the principal's office, but Principal John Collins was sympathetic and let him return to class. After school, James returned to the hotel, only to meet Erica Slaughter in the parking lot. She informed him that she would believe anything he said, and prompted him to describe the monster he saw. He realizes that she is a monster hunter, and asks if he can assist her in killing it.
