# Archer's Peak

Archer's Peak 

 Information 

 Location 
 Wisconsin, USA 

 Archer's Peak is a small town in Wisconsin. Due to the manifestations of James , the town was plagued by Oscuratype monsters for several weeks, forcing the Order of St. George to intervene. 

 History 
 Archer's Peak was considered a normal, small town for a long while, with few incidents happening other than hunting accidents. However, in 2020, after young resident James made up a story of a monster to scare his friends, the story spread and the collective fear summoned an Oscuratype creature that began to kill the local children. It would eventually start collecting bodies to bring to its den, where it would then give birth to five children .
