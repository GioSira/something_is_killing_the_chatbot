 Ms. Mahoney is the mother of Tommy and Sophie Mahoney , the latter of whom was kidnapped and killed by an Oscuratype monster . Distraught over the death, Ms. Mahoney became angry, suicidal, and addicted to cigarettes. Her son, Tommy, would later take responsibility for the deaths in order to protect her and the rest of the town from being killed by the House of Slaughter .
