# Necrotype

| “ | Hunter beware, destroying the possessed human corpses of the Phantasma-Necrotype complicates any hunt, as the corpse's living relatives must never discover the truth. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Butcher |  |

**Necrotypes** are a breed of monster fought by The Order of St. George. Commonly referred to as "zombies," they are the manifestation of the undead and revitalized corpses.

## History

The Necrotype first comes from Haitian folklore, where the fear of being forced into slavery after death manifested the idea of zombies. The proliferation of Zombie media would then spread the fears across the globe, causing Necrotypes to actually form.

## Description

Necrotypes usually follow the regular appearances, strengths and weaknesses of the common media zombie.

### Classifications

There are two types of Necrotypes: Common and Phantasma.

- The **Common Necrotype** is a manifestation of a fear of the undead. They must be killed according to the fear that spawned them; traditionally, this involves the destruction of the body.
- The **Phantasma-Necrotype** is when a Phantasmatype possesses a corpse, usually a human one. These corpses then become the Phantasmatype's tether, which must be destroyed to release the spirit.
