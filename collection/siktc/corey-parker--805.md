# Corey Parker

**Corey Parker** was a teenage boy living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin with his three siblings during the Archer's Peak incident of 2020. While with his family to identify the remains of his third sibling, he and his other brothers were attacked and killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype children.

## History

Corey was the older brother of Brendan and Jason Parker, as well as another unnamed sibling. He claims that he checked the family computer's history once to find out Jason was watching hentai.

At some point, the boys' third sibling was taken and killed by a monster. When their parents were asked to identify the body, Jason requested they "tag along." Once in the parking lot of the school, Jason ran into the woods, with his brothers following after him. Corey complained about this, before spotting blood on the ground. Tommy Mahoney then burst from the bushes, telling them to run. Corey saw one of the monsters, asking his brother if he could see it too, before being sliced in half by one of the Archer's Peak Oscuratype children.

His and his siblings' deaths would later be blamed on Tommy Mahoney.

## Physical appearance

Corey was a young Caucasian boy with light brown eyes and black hair.
