# Alyssa Dean

**Alyssa Dean** was an unseen girl who lived in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the 2020 Archer's Peak incident. She was killed by the Oscuratype Mother, with her body being found in its den.

## History

Little is known about Alyssa Dean. At some point in 2020, she was killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother. Her body would be found in its den, and her family called to the school gymnasium to identify her.
