# Cecilia Slaughter

> I haven't been a Black Mask in a very long time. But I trained side by side with the woman who taught you everything you know. If took me less than an *hour* to find you after reaching Tribulation. And if you think there is any place in this rotten world you could hide from me... then you aren't as smart as I *know* you are.
>
> —Cecilia Slaughter to Erica Slaughter, *Something Is Killing the Children* #32

**Cecilia Slaughter** is the Head of the White Masks of the House of Slaughter, under the Order of St. George. The next-in-line to be the Dragon of the House of Slaughter, Cecilia was formerly a Black Mask trainee before switching into the White Masks and quickly rising in position to the House Council.

## Physical appearance

Cecilia is a young Caucasian woman with red eyes and short, white hair.
