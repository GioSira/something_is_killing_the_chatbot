# Jason Parker

**Jason Parker** was a teenage boy living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin with his three siblings during the Archer's Peak incident of 2020. While with his family to identify the remains of his third sibling, he and his other brothers were attacked and killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype children.

## History

Jason was the younger brother of Corey and Brendan Parker, and the brother of a third unnamed sibling. Corey claims that he watches hentai on the family computer.

At some point, the boys' third sibling was taken and killed by a monster. When their parents were asked to identify the body, Jason requested they "tag along." Once in the parking lot of the school, Jason ran into the woods, with his brothers following after him. Unfortunately, Jason was soon killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype children. The brief appearance of his corpse shows that at least one of his legs was ripped off, and his intestines were pulled from his chest.

His and his siblings' deaths would later be blamed on Tommy Mahoney.

## Physical appearance

Not much of Jason's corpse is seen, or his physical appearance known. Brendan describes him as shorter than his brothers, and wearing gym shorts.
