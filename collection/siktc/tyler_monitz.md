 Tyler Monitz was a young boy living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin in 2020. A friend of James , Noah and Karl , Tyler was killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother while at a sleepover with his friends.

 History 
 Tyler was the older brother of Carly Monitz and the best friend of James , though this seemed to be a recent development as James remarked that he'd never had "sleepover friends" before Tyler. The two of them were also close friends with Noah ; when James expressed a crush on Karl , they encouraged him to ask him out. During one sleepover, they tried to sneak out of James's house, only to get caught. James would later tell Tyler the story of a monster he supposedly saw near the ravine. This frightened Tyler, and he then used this story to scare his sister and her friends at their own slumber party, resulting in the story spreading enough to form the monster in reality. 
 A week later, Tyler played truth-or-dare with James, Noah and Karl at a sleepover. When James picked "Truth," Tyler asked him what the most scared he'd ever been was, prompting him to tell the story now to Noah and Karl. Noah called bullshit on the story, so James dared him to go look for himself. All four boys would go to the ravine to look under the bridge for the creature, with Tyler complaining that he was stepping in deer shit. 
 The monster would then materialize and attack the boys. Tyler was the last one to die, as a separated James rejoined the group and discovered the carnage. Tyler begged James for help as he was impaled from behind by the Oscuratype's claw, before being ripped in half.
 While the deaths would be blamed on a bear originally, people suspected James of killing his friends for a while. A memorial was placed up for Tyler and the other boys at Archer's Peak High School . Eventually, his death would be blamed on Tommy Mahoney .
