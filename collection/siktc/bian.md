 Bian is a young girl from Archer's Peak, Wisconsin who was a survivor of the 2020 Archer's Peak incident . Surviving an Oscuratype attack by playing dead, Bian would become protective of the monster's children due to their perceived innocence. When the children started to grow and attack, Bian assisted Erica Slaughter and James in summoning and killing them.

 History 
 Bian lived in Archer's Peak , Wisconsin, with her father and older sister . Her father did not like the girls much, and it's suggested that they had not been living with him long, as Bian says she wasn't sure about this. When Bian's Sister was invited to Carly Monitz 's sleepover, Bian was forced to go along as well so that her father wouldn't have to deal with her. At the party, Tyler Monitz interrupted the girls' scary stories to talk about the monster his friend James supposedly saw in the ravine. Effectively frightened, the girls started spreading the story around school to prevent other children from going into the woods.
 However, soon, children began to disappear. The more children that vanished, the more frightened Bian became, and she began believing she could see the monster in the forest. The monster eventually came to her house and ate her sister. Bian pretended to be dead, and the monster brought her body to its den. Bian remained there for a long time, as her father did not report either girl missing and apparently skipped town soon after. Bian watched the monster "get big" and give birth to five baby monsters , which she was not afraid of, unlike their mother. The mother would eat living children, and bring dead ones for her own babies to feast on. Bian said this was not the babies' fault, as they were only eating what their mother brought them. She also later referred to the babies as "nice," implying that she had positive interactions with them when their mother was away. 
 When Erica Slaughter and Tommy Mahoney entered the monster's den, about a month-and-a-half into the Incident, Bian heard the adults speaking and managed to approach them from where she was hiding in the pile of children's body parts. Erica would stab Tommy Mahoney with Horde Gold, enabling him to see the monster, before passing Bian and an injured James to him to escape with. He would run them to a nearby car, where Officer John Richards was recovering consciousness. Bian simply told him that the monster didn't eat her, and that she was cold; on the drive to the hospital, Richards promised to turn the heat on for her. 
 After Bian's injuries were assessed, she was taken to the police station to be questioned. She refused to give her last name, as she liked the station better than her home. She also warned Officer Richards that moving the bodies would mean the babies would go hungry, but she could tell he didn't believe her. Staying in the waiting room with Gail , the secretary, Bian would spend her time drawing. Erica eventually approached her to ask about the babies, and Bian would tell her what she had noticed in the cave. She would also show her her drawings of the babies, and of Erica killing the mother. 
 That night, Bian would ask Gail if she had ever seen a dead person. When Gail responded that she had seen her father die, Bian then asked if anything had tried to eat his body. Erica then returned with Aaron Slaughter , and the two of them attempted to take Bian; Erica told her that she could take her drawing pad, and Bian also requested the markers, as she had never had so many colors. At that, John Richards entered with a gun drawn, telling Bian she could have all the drawing materials if she came with him. When Gail attempted to call the Sheriff , Aaron knocked her out, frightening and confusing Bian. Richards then told Bian that the hunters planned to use her as bait; she was indifferent to this, but more concerned about them trying to kill the baby monsters. Erica then knocked out Aaron and passed her off to Richards, telling him to hide her somewhere safe. 
 Richards took Bian to his parents' house, which was empty as they were visiting his sister. Bian remained in an old room, coloring, until Erica reappeared. After Erica explained that the babies had grown up and begun to kill people, Bian became sad, saying she wanted them to stay small and nice. She then told Erica about when she had first heard about the monsters, confirming James was the manifester to the hunter. Erica left to comfort James, just as the White Masks of the House of Slaughter arrived, capturing everyone in the house and bringing them to the back porch. Bian, holding Erica's backpack, ran to her as Cecilia Slaughter slit Richards's throat. Cecilia threatened to take the children to complete the ritual, but a dying Richards tried to shoot her, providing enough distraction for Erica to sneak away with the children. Bian asked if Richards was dead, and upon hearing he was, said, "That's too bad. He let me bring all the colors."
 At James's House, Erica told the children that they were going to do something dangerous and scary, but it would lure in the remaining monsters. She then had Octo reveal his real face to them, which frightened Bian. Erica then had the children play the Game of Nowhere , lying down and picturing images in their head to manifest into Octo. After James described the monster's creation, Bian began to cry, saying that he was talking about the Mama, who wasn't nice like the babies. Erica told her to imagine the monster Tyler had told her about, causing Octo to take its form. Octo called out for the monster's children, summoning them to James's yard. Erica left James to watch Bian as she killed the remnants of the litter outside. Erica would eventually return to the house, silently dousing her head in cold water. She then called the House of Slaughter and reported the monsters dead, and asked James if she could use his shower.
 A week later, the police were still unable to find Bian's family, and she was adopted by Gail. Her capture and her sister's death was blamed on Tommy Mahoney . The last time Bian is seen, she is drawing on Gail's floor. 
 In his conversation with Erica, the Old Dragon suggested that Erica could have recruited Bian into the Order of St. George . An angry Erica replied that would have only happened if Bian was willing to come, and if not, she would have had to kill her. 

 Physical appearance 
 Bian appears to be of Eastern Asian descent; her name suggests either Japanese or Vietnamese origin. She has short-cut black hair and green eyes. She has a gap between her front teeth, and a scar across her face, from above her right eye, moving downwards diagonally, with another scar on the bottom right of her lip. Bia is very skinny, with a long, narrow face.
 When in the monster's cave, she is in an oversized pink tank top and light blue leggings. Upon being rescued, she is dressed in a teal hoodie, gray skirt, light blue socks, and black Mary Jane shoes.

 Personality 
 Bian is very blunt and direct, as well as seemingly unaffected by death and gore; whether this is due to her abduction or a prior history of abuse is unknown. While discussing the monsters' eating habits, she said that the children were already dead, so they might as well feed the babies. She is not completely indifferent, though, as she is frightened by the House of Slaughter and Octo 's demonic form and protective of the monster's children. The latter also shows her care and affection for the innocent, as she doesn't blame the terrifying creatures for what happened to her nor for eating what their mother brought them. She did, however, eventually help destroy them after they started attacking other children. 
 It is suggested she came from a difficult home life, as she preferred being in the police station waiting room to being at home. Possibly because of this, she is able to notice things many other people would overlook, even in stressful situations. She is very clever and quick-witted, as she was able to play dead well enough to deceive the Oscuratype Mother, and then to survive in its lair for several days. She enjoys art and drawing, and especially loves colored markers.

 Trivia 
 "Bian" as a name can have multiple meanings:
 In Japanese, it can be formed from multiple kanji combinations. Most popularly, the bi can be from either 美, meaning "beautiful," or 弥, meaning "universally." The an could come from 安 ("peace, quiet"), 案 ("plan"), 杏 ("apricot") or 晏 ("peaceful, quiet, clear, late in the day"). 
 In Vietnamese, it is a name that means "secret." It is, however, rarely used as a given name.
