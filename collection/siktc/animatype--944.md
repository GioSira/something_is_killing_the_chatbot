# Animatype

| “ | Humans have spent generations studying this planet's great predators and, for the most part, those same methods apply to their monstrum counterparts. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Butcher |  |

**Animatypes** are a breed of monster fought by The Order of St. George. They originate from fears of beasts and other animal or insect life, and can usually be killed in similar ways to their natural counterparts.

## History

Animatypes are likely the second type of monster to form in History, following the Oscuratype. They originate from fears of animals, and usually appear in very specific locations, often those with extreme environments.

In America, the ursine (bear) Animatypes are the most common in the North, and alligators in the South.

## Description

They originate from fears of animals, and usually take the heightened form of the animal that inspired it. They are considered to be the easiest of monsters to hunt and kill, as they assume the characteristics of the animal which inspired it. Thus, they are as easy to kill as those animals.

### Classifications

- **Animatype Normalis** - an Animatype that appears in an expected environment (i.e. alligators in a swamp).
- **Animatype Anomalia** - an Animatype that appears in an environment unexpected for their species (i.e. sharks in freshwater).
- **Animatype Fabulosa** - an Animatype that is more fantastic than realistic in regards to its appearance and powerset, usually brought on by local legends and cryptids. These mainly appear as winged creatures, such as pterosaurs. When their variegation differs too much from a regular Animatype, they are recategorized as Mythotypes.
