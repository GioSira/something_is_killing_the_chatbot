# Mythotype

| “ | Mythotype monsters are culturally specific and are generally contained within their region of origin; however, as the world's populations intermixed, folklore spread and many Mythotypes prospered in new locales. This is especially relevant to nations with large immigrant populations, like Canada and the United States, where migrants brought their monsters with them. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Butcher |  |

**Mythotypes** are a breed of monster fought by The Order of St. George. They are a miscellaneous group of folklore-inspired monsters that are not categorized as Chimeratype, Nosferatype or Necrotype.

## History

Mythotype monsters are usually very culturally-specific and contained within certain locations. However, immigrant populations caused mythology to spread and thus caused the spread of these Mythotypes. The tradition of scary stories around a campfire, during sleepovers, and through horror media caused the Mythotype to become the second-most populated type of monster, following the Oscuratype.

## Description

Mythotypes are described as any folklore-specific creature that isn't a Chimeratype, Nosferatype, or Necrotype. The former two had previously been considered Mythotypes before their popularity caused them to be considered a separate species.

### Classifications

- **Baba Yaga**
- **Demons**
- **Faeries**
- **Mothman**
