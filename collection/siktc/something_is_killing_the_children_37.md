 Something Is Killing the Children #36 
 Something Is Killing the Children #38 

 Something Is Killing the Children #37 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on May 22, 2024, by Boom! Studios . It is the thirty-seventh issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series, and the second issue of the five-part Road Stories arc.

 Synopsis 
 Years before the foundational Archer's Peak Saga, this standalone story continues the formative look at Erica Slaughter 's early days before she became the hunter she is now!
 When encountering a species of monster she's unfamiliar with killing, what lessons will this young hunter learn as she faces a foe that could finally outwit her?
