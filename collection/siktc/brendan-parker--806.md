# Brendan Parker

> They already see us! They're already trying to kill us! They killed my brothers.
>
> —Brendan Parker , *Something Is Killing the Children* #9

**Brendan Parker** was a teenage boy living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin with his three siblings during the Archer's Peak incident of 2020. While with his family to identify the remains of his third sibling, he and his other brothers were attacked and killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype children.

## History

Brendan was the younger brother of Corey Parker and the elder brother of Jason. The boys also had a third, unnamed sibling who was taken and killed by a monster. When their parents were asked to identify the body, Jason requested they "tag along." Once in the parking lot of the school, Jason ran into the woods, with his brothers following after him. Brendan kept a cooler head than the upset Corey, until Corey spotted blood on the ground. Tommy Mahoney then burst from the bushes, screaming for the children to run. Brendan began to ask him if he'd seen Jason, only for Corey to then ask Brendan if he could see the approaching monsters.

Brendan then witnessed his brother be sliced in half by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype children, before they were surrounded by the creatures. Brendan cried for help, despite Tommy warning him to be quiet, terrified and traumatized by the deaths of his brothers. While Tommy attempted to distract the monsters, Brendan tried to run, only to be speared by an Oscuratype claw and near-instantly killed. Tommy was found by the police holding his body, but his arrest was interrupted by another Oscuratype kill in the parking lot. Nevertheless, Tommy would be blamed for Brendan's and his sibling's murders.

## Physical appearance

Brendan was a young Caucasian boy with light brown eyes and hair.
