# Emerald Mask

**Emerald Masks** are a branch of The Order of St. George who don the color green. They are the highest-ranking of all other masks except for the House Dragons and possibly Violet Masks, though that is currently unclear, due to the fact they have been trained to hunt and kill Dragons, the most vicious and deadly of all Monsters. With the slow decline of Dragons appearing in the modern world, Emerald Masks are very rare, and usually found in countries with deep dragon folklore.

The Emerald Masks are the Dragon-Hunters of each house. Becoming an Emerald Mask is a difficult task, and requires extreme training and discipline. As Dragons are the most dangerous of all Monsters, those who hunt Dragons have to be just as dangerous. As the founder of the Order, St. George himself, began the organization after slaying a dragon, this branch is extremely well-regarded and respected among all other members.

There are two ways to earn an Emerald Mask. The first is to find and kill a dragon in the wild; all dragon-killers will automatically be regarded as Emeralds. However, with the lack of dragons, most of the remaining Emerald Masks gain their color by first training as a Silver Mask. They then have to become a Master at that rank, and then go to the Great House in order to train in secret for multiple years.

Many houses no longer have Emerald Masks, simply due to a lack of need for them.
