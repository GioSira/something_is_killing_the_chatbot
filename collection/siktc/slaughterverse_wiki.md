# Slaughterverse Wiki

Slaughterverse: The World of Something Is Killing the Children 

 It's been a year since Erica Slaughter was last seen in Archer's Peak . Now, the hunter has resurfaced to take on the case of a girl who's seen a new kind of monster , one with terrifying implications... But that's not all she'll have to contend with. Erica has broken ties with the House of Slaughter , and that can have deadly consequences. The Order of St. George does not forget and they do not forgive. While Erica goes on the hunt for her deadliest foe yet, a mysterious figure follows closely behind... bringing with them a storm like she has never seen before!
 On September 4, 2019, Boom! Studios published Something Is Killing the Children by creators James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera . In addition to the spin-off House of Slaughter and various one-shot comics such as Book of Slaughter that followed, Something Is Killing the Children inspired a television series being produced on Netflix by Dark and 1899 creators Baran bo Odar and Jantje Friese .
