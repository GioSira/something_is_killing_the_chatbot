# Azure Mask

**Azure Masks** are a branch of The Order of St. George who don the color blue and work as spymasters within society. Originally a branch dedicated to the weaponeers of the Order, with the advent of modern technology, they have evolved into being hackers, programmers, planners, and investigators. Unlike other Masks, members of this branch sometimes keep their own surnames and live outside the House.

## Description

| “ | Azure Masks are the weaponeers of the Order. In ancient times, they crafted the tools by which hunters would hunt and kill the monsters they faced in the wild. In modern times, where acquiring deadly weapons is not a matter of great difficulty, the Azure Masks have become the spymasters of the Order. It is their duty above all others to prevent the wider world from learning of the existence of monsters and the machinations of the Order of St. George. They infiltrate law enforcement, politics, and news organizations at their highest levels, keeping our great secrets intact. | ” |

In the early days of the Order, the Azure Mask was the color given to the blacksmiths and weapon makers. In modern times, now that weapons are more easy to come by, the color denotes those who work in espionage and various other forms of undercover work. Most Azure Masks find themselves in tech departments, where they suppress news of monster attacks from hitting social media, create stories to cover up incidents, and hack into dangerous systems. Their most important task is to keep monsters' existence hidden from the public. Not all Azures do this, though; many also infiltrate law enforcement, journalism, governments, and the inner circles of the rich in order to get what they need.

Azures will often live outside of the House, with some placed on special locations to help cover up training exercises, and others allowed to integrate into society to alleviate suspicion. In the case of the latter, many Azures are allowed to keep their surname and join or start a family outside of the Order, a privilege denied to other branches. As such, lineage recruits are common in this line. Some Azures are so separate from the Order, only doing one or two tasks in their lives, that they stop believing in monsters altogether.

While the number of Azures in a House is usually small, they often work with Scarlet Masks, as the only other branch of non-hunters. Some White Masks look down on Azures as loners and weaklings.
