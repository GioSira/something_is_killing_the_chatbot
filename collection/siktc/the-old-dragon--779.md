# The Old Dragon

**The Old Dragon** is the Dragon of the House of Slaughter, under The Order of St. George. He is an elderly man who rules his House through careful planning and manipulation, and has a special interest in Erica Slaughter.

## Physical appearance

The Old Dragon is an elderly man with a long white beard whose right eye is damaged.
