# Sophie Mahoney

**Sophie Mahoney** was a 12-year-old girl living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the 2020 Archer's Peak incident. She was kidnapped and killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother, which used her body parts to feed its children. Her disappearance would prompt her brother, Tommy, to investigate Erica Slaughter's hunt.

## Physical appearance

Sophie was a young 12-year-old Caucasian girl.
