 Something Is Killing the Children #31 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on July 19, 2023 by Boom! Studios . It is the thirty-first issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series. 

 Synopsis 
 ERICA VS CUTTER ! The Final Chapter of Tribulation Begins Here! After narrowly surviving the Duplicitype , Erica Slaughter must still reckon with the murderous Cutter in order to save the people of Tribulation. Separated from Dolly and driven by desperate rage, Cutter won't stop until she exacts her revenge on Erica.
