# Victor Saint-Georges

> Adults underestimate children, I've found. They are meant to be protectors, but often they fail to do this in the face of threats they do not understand. And then they can be harsh when they try to show they are still in control. But children can often tell when the adults are not in control. And they will choose to act in their own interest.
>
> —Victor Saint-Georges , *Something Is Killing the Children* #0

**Victor Saint-Georges** is a Violet Mask monster hunter within the Great House of St. George, under the order of the same name. He is a mysterious man interested in Erica Slaughter's recruitment to the Great House.

## History

At some point, Victor was recruited into the Order of St. George, and inducted into the Great House to serve under the Dragon King. He became a Violet Mask, specializing in spy work.

Victor had an interest in recruiting Erica Slaughter to the Great House, and followed her work for some time. After her departure from the House of Slaughter, he kept tabs on her, and eventually went to interview Sara, a young girl she had helped; at the same time, he disposed of her parents and drugged her ice cream so that she could later be recruited into the organization. Amazed that she was able to use Sara without making her feel unsafe, Victor decided that it was time to put Erica through the Trials.

Soon afterwards, he would meet with Cecilia Slaughter per her request at the House of Cutter. She would request the Great House's blessing to expand the House of Slaughter to San Francisco, Washington D.C., Seoul and Dubai. Victor would agree under the condition that should Erica Slaughter turn out to be alive following her fight with the Duplicitype, she would be under the custody of the Great House rather than Slaughter.

## Physical appearance

Victor appears to be of European descent, with pale skin and blond hair cut very short. He has blue eyes, though his left eye is swollen shut with a large and intimidating scar.
