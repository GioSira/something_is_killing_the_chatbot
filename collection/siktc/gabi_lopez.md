 Gabriella "Gabi" Lopez was a young preteen girl living in Tribulation, New Mexico in 2021 during the Duplicitype attacks. Having accidentally summoned the Erica Duplicitype via her own fears, Gabi witnessed it killing her entire family, before teaming up with Erica Slaughter and Riqui in order to kill it. While laying a trap for the Duplicitype, Gabi and Riqui were caught by Charlotte Cutter , who poisoned Gabi in order to lure Erica closer to her. Seeing that Erica had her way of seeing the monster cut off, Gabi allowed herself to be bitten by the creature in order to finally kill it, dying of both the poison and blood loss as a result.

 Physical appearance 
 Gabi is a preteen latina girl with dark skin and blue eyes. She has very short hair, swept over one half of her head while the other half is shaved; her hair is also dyed a bright green and pink. The exception to her short hair is one long strand, which she keeps braided over her right ear. She usually wears jackets and jeans.

 Personality 
 Gabi was a very troubled child, who would often snap at adults and authority figures. She was very rebellious and argumentative, and usually would pick fights with her grandmother, who was more religious than she was and took offense to Gabi's cussing out of God and graffiti on portraits of Jesus. She often swears, usually telling people to "fuck off," and acts confrontational with strangers. She did not have a good relationship with the family cat, and was often rude to Riqui, who would watch over her family. Riqui said that Gabi would hide her emotions via her anger and frustration, which turned out to be true as Gabi claimed to be unafraid of death despite that not being true.
 Despite her anger issues, Gabi loved her family dearly, and was devastated upon finding them dead. She also became protective of Erica, dying in order to stop the monster that was hunting her. As she died, she admitted she was afraid, but that she needed to see her family and apologize to her grandmother for what she'd said about God. 
 Gabi had a fear of humanity that manifested in the Erica Duplicitype , which began hunting and killing children around Tribulation. It is unknown what exactly caused this fear, but she had an absentee father, her family was described as "insane", and she would often accuse people around her of being pedophiles or otherwise acting sexually, suggesting she may have some trauma surrounding that. She sometimes derogatorally refers to Riqui and other women caring for her as "gay."
