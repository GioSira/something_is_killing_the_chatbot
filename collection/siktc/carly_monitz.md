 Carly Monitz is the younger sister of Tyler Monitz , one of the victims of the monster attacks in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the 2020 incident .

 History 
 Carly Monitz was the younger sister of Tyler , who liked to scare her. Carly invited the older sister of Bian to her sleepover, which Bian was forced to tag along to; it's also implied that Sara Washington was at the slumber party. While at the party with many other girls, Tyler interrupted their scary stories with one of his own, the one of James seeing a monster. Frightened, Carly and her friends told everyone in school the story in order to keep them from wandering into the woods.
