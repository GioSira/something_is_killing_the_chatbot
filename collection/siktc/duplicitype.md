# Duplicitype

“

 These monstrum are specific in their replica, as they are inspired by human danger and take the shape of known individuals. 

 ”

 — The Book of Butcher 
 Duplicitypes are a breed of monster fought by The Order of St. George . They are spawned from fears of specific humans, such as an abuser or dangerous criminal, and will take the form of their inspiration. However, across their life, they can change their form, and other forms of Duplicitype do exist.

 History 
 The Duplicitype is born from a human's fear of other humans, and that of the danger one human can pose to another. The most common form of Duplicitype is that which takes the form of a child's abuser, though fears of mass murderers and wanted criminals can also create these monsters. 

 Description 
 Duplicitypes usually form in the image of the human that inspired the fear that created them, but in a hazy, incomplete way. With each kill, the Duplicitype grows stronger, and is able to improve their replication. When they become near-identical to a human, they start to gain sentience and the ability to communicate. Common Duplicitypes have a large eyeball in their gut, presumed to be their means of scanning and replication. 

 Classifications 
 Duplicitype Oculus - the common form of a Duplicitype. They form from fears of other, specific humans. 
 Duplicitype Doppelgänger - a Duplicitype that forms from a fear of self. They will take their creator's image and feed off their fear until they have a perfect replica, to which they will devour the original human and assume their place. 
 Duplicitype Changeling - a rare but sad form of the Duplicitype that forms from the fears of new parents. One of the few monsters that human adults can see from inception, the monster will kill and replace the newborn, before eating their caretakers.
