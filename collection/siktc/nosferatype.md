 Nosferatypes are a breed of monster fought by The Order of St. George . These monsters are those that feed on blood, fluids, or other energies of their victims, rather than simple meat.

 History 
 While vampiric monsters have always existeed across the globe, the modern Nosferatype comes mainly from Eatern European folklore. The Nosferatypes were known to be incredibly powerful during the Bubonic Plague of the Middle Ages, which in turn caused them to often be associated with rats. 
 They are culturally considered the most famous of all monsters; however, recent sanitizations and reinterpretations of vampires in pop culture has dramatically decreased their populations. They were originally classified as Mythotypes before their popularity shifted them into their own category. 

 Description 
 Nosferatypes will not feed on the flesh of their victims, like the other types of monsters, but on other bodily fluids, usually blood or energy. Their victims will often be found perfectly intact, but emptied dry. Common Vampires will feed on blood instead of flesh, but other designations have other appetites.

 Classifications 
 Common Vampire - feeds on blood. 
 Psychic Vampire - feeds on guilt, trauma, anger, despair, and other dark emotions. 
 Succubus/Incubus - feed on sexual energies and fluids.
