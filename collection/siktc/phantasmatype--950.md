# Phantasmatype

| “ | The most insidious of all monstrum also happens to the Order of St. George's greatest asset in the war against monsterkind, and that is the Phantasmatype. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Butcher |  |

**Phantasmatypes** are a breed of monster fought by The Order of St. George. Coming from the fear and grief of death and tragedy, the Phantasmatype is an incredibly dangerous type, but also the ones that form the Order's sacred totems.

## History

Little is known about the origins of Phantasmatypes, though they usually come from grief surrounding the recently deceased. Any monster that is made to possess an object becomes a Phantasmatype despite its previous classifications.

## Description

The Phantasmatype is most often known to locale as "ghosts" or "spirits." They have fully non-corporeal forms, but hold a material tether that binds them to reality and gives them the ability to hunt and haunt. These tethers must be severed in order to fully destroy the Phantasmatype, usually through breakage or destruction. However, tethers can be anything, such as a house, ancient relic, or living being.
