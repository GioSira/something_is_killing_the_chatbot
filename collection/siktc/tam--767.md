# Tam

**Tam** is a young waitress at Applebeams, who worked alongside Tommy Mahoney, her manager at Applebeams. She was minorly involved in Erica Slaughter's monster hunt.

## Physical appearance

Tam is a young African American women with brown eyes, shoulder-length red hair, and glasses.
