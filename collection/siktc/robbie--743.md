# Robbie

> But *Robbie* never did *shit* to you! He never even called you a goddamn name.
>
> —Dan Brisby to James, *Something Is Killing the Children* #1

**Robbie** is a young boy who attended Archer's Peak High School. He was a victim of the Oscuratype Mother in the Archer's Peak incident.

## History

Little is known about Robbie, other than he was friendly with both Dan Brisby and James. Dan remarks that Robbie was never cruel to James. Dan accuses James of killing Robbie for not liking him back romantically.

Robbie died during the Archer's Peak incident, with his head being found detached from his body. While for a while his cause of killer was unknown, it was later discovered to be an attack from an Oscuratype Mother. A memorial for him was placed outside Archer's Peak High School along with ones for Tyler Monitz, Noah and Karl.
