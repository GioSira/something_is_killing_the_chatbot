# Karl

**Karl** was a young boy living in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin in 2020. A friend of James, Noah and Tyler Monitz, Karl was killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother while at a sleepover with his friends.

## History

Karl was a new addition to James's friend group, but James talked about him enough that Noah would forget he hadn't been with them. Noah and Tyler Monitz encouraged James to ask Karl out, as James had a crush on him. While Karl didn't reciprocate, he did say they should be "just friends" and would hang out with all three of the boys.

While Karl didn't reciprocate James's affections, it is implied that he is indeed gay or queer, as he jokes to James that Tyler is cute.

At a sleepover with James, Noah and Tyler, Tyler prompted James to tell the story of a monster he'd seen in the woods. When Noah calls bullshit, James has everyone go to the ravine to look under the bridge for it. Noah pokes fun at James's crush on Karl, and Karl tells James not to ask "those two" for advice on romance. At the bottom of the hill, Noah spots a monster, which attacks the boys. When James, who had been separated from them, returned to the ravine, he found Karl's body with his jaw, arms, and at least one leg ripped off.

While the deaths would be blamed on a bear originally, people suspected James of killing his friends for a while. A memorial was placed up for Karl and the other boys at Archer's Peak High School. Eventually, his death would be blamed on Tommy Mahoney.

## Physical appearance

Karl was a young Caucasian boy with blond hair and glasses.
