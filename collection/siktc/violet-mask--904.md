# Violet Mask

**Violet Masks** are a branch of The Order of St. George who don the color purple. They only exist within The Great House of St. George, where they exclusively serve the Dragon King and work as his messengers. The route to become a Violet Mask is unknown, but it is known that they are not to be messed with.

## Description

| “ | The Dragon King does not leave the Great House of St. George, and instead sends his emissaries to enforce his rule among the Congregation of Houses. His emissaries are masked in violet, and are sent to oversee the transition between Dragons, resolve territorial disputes between Houses, and to revoke House charters. The requirements to become a Violet Mask are shrouded in secrecy, and it is said that they know the answers to some of the most fundamental truths that underlie our organization's great work. In any case, it is known that they are all extremely dangerous. | ” |
| --- | --- | --- |
|  | — The Book of Slaughter |  |

Little is known about the mysterious Violet Masks. They only exist within The Great House of St. George in Northern Africa, where they work as messengers and diplomats on behalf of The Great Dragon. They are known to relay his orders, oversee disputes between Houses, and, on occasion, order the destruction of a House. They are also said to know many of the Order's secrets. It is unknown how one becomes a Violet Mask.

## Known Violet Masks

The only known house to host Violet Masks is the Great House of St. George. Victor Saint-Georges is currently the only named Violet Mask, though he may have recruited a young girl named Sara; it is unknown if she survived or if this would put her on the Violet Mask path.
