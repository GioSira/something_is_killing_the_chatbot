 Something Is Killing the Children #37 
 Something Is Killing the Children #39 

 Something Is Killing the Children #38 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on June 26, 2024, by Boom! Studios . It is the thirty-eighth issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series, and the third issue of the five-part Road Stories arc.

 Synopsis 
 During the third vicious escapade into Erica 's secret past, she finds herself squaring off with a monster worse than nightmares...
 However, as she struggles to live another day after yet another battle, it becomes clear that an even more deadly monster lies at the heart of it all, and it will take all the new skills she's acquired to track it down...
