# Sally

**Sally** was an unseen girl who lived in Archer's Peak, Wisconsin during the 2020 Archer's Peak incident. She would be captured and killed by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother.

## History

Little is known about Sally. She was the daughter of a woman named Victoria. At some point, she would be either killed or captured and then murdered by the Archer's Peak Oscuratype mother, and her body would be found inside its den.
