# House of Slaughter 4

**House of Slaughter #4** is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on January 26, 2022 by Boom! Studios. It is the fourth issue in the *House of Slaughter* comic series.

## Synopsis

Jace embarks on the life or death trial that all White Masks must undergo... the Ritual Hunt. Meanwhile, Aaron is torn between his burgeoning feelings for Jace and his duty to the House, but not for long -- if Jessica Slaughter has anything to say about it.

## Characters

- Aaron Slaughter
- Jace Boucher
- Jessica Slaughter
