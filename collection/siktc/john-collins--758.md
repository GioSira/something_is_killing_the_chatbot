# John Collins

> I'm a mess. They should fire me. I was just *in* school a couple years ago. I don't know what the fuck I'm doing here.
>
> —Principal John Collins , *Something Is Killing the Children* #1

**John Collins** is the principal of Archer's Peak High School. He became unwittingly involved in the Archer's Peak incident when the police needed to commandeer the gymnasium as a makeshift morgue, which later had a crowd of people locked inside in order to avoid mysterious monsters.

## Physical appearance

Principal Collins is a young African American man with brown eyes and black hair.
