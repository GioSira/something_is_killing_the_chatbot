 My mistress doesn't like when people take her things. She might have let you live, but not she'll hunt you down and rip each of you apart. 
 —Dolly to Gabi Lopez , Something Is Killing the Children #30 

 Dolly was a Phantasmatype monster bound to Charlotte Cutter of the House of Cutter . She served as a Totem for her master and was very beloved by her, being highly protected and defended against anyone who so much as slighted her. After the death of Charlotte Cutter, it is unknown what happened to Dolly, but she was most likely destroyed as is tradition.
