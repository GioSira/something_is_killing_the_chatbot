 Something Is Killing the Children #5 is a horror comic from James Tynion IV and Werther Dell'Edera that was published on January 29, 2020 by Boom! Studios . It is the fifth issue in the Something Is Killing the Children comic series, the final issue of the five-part The Angel of Archer's Peak arc, and the fifth issue of the fifteen-part Archer's Peak saga.

 Synopsis 
 The time for investigation is over. The hunt begins, as Erica Slaughter brings her namesake to the monsters of Archer's Peak. 
 Plot 
 Sitting by the ridge, James sees a ripped-apart deer carcass. He flashes back to his dead friends, only to then realize he can hear Octo taunting him. Octo convinces him to take the weapons she gave him and help her in order to stop her from dying like James's friends.
 In the den, Erica orders Tommy Mahoney to step back. Not able to see the monster behind him, he keeps threatening her with his gun. She revs up the chainsaw, tells him to "get the fuck out of her way," and begins fighting the monster. Seeing her slice the air and blood appearing seemingly out of nowhere, Tommy cries that Erica killed his sister and attempts to shoot her. She says that he just made the beast angry, and he cries that there is nothing there. They stop their fight, though, as they realize the bullet hit James, who had just entered. Erica throws James's jacket to distract the monster, who goes after its scent; she's assessed that it's blind. 
 She tells Tommy to shut up and help her save James; they then hear a living child, Bian , who emerges from the pile of bodies, having played dead after the monster took her and her sister. Tommy finds the mangled remains of his sister, about to have a breakdown. Knowing that she needs him to understand in order to save James and Bian, Erica breaks a major rule and stabs his head with Horde Gold, giving him the ability to see monsters. This alerts the House of Slaughter and The Old Dragon of what Erica has done. 
 Erica orders Tommy to take James and Bian outside; now able to see what she's fighting, Erica promises him that she will recover Sophie's body if they survive. Tommy carries the children out, promising them that nobody is going to die, while Erica stays behind to slay the monster. He manages to reach Officer Richards , who has just awoken from being knocked out. Confused but understanding the situation is deadly, Richards drives everyone to St. Mary's Hospital . James asks Richards to "tell the octopus" that he doesn't want to die. 
 Erica returns to her pack, answering a call from Aaron Slaughter . She said she did what she had to do, and will deal with the consequences, but that he didn't classify the monster correctly, and that she was in a nest instead of a food stash. This means that there are monster children running wild, hungry, and afraid, and that they are going to have to kill them quickly in order to save the town.
